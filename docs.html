How-to: common tasks

Connect CarPlay or Android Auto
  1. 2022–2024: plug the phone into the USB data port (not the charge-only port) with a data cable. Allow CarPlay on the phone when asked.
  2. 2025+: for wireless, pair the phone over Bluetooth first, then turn on Phone Projection in the screen's device connection settings. The menu names vary a little by software version.
  3. If it doesn't appear, check Phone Projection settings on the car, and on iPhone check Settings → General → CarPlay.
Update maps and software (USB)
  1. Install the Navigation Updater from update.hyundai.com on your Mac or PC.
  2. Sign in, choose your IONIQ 5 and model year, and download the update to a blank USB drive.
  3. With the car on (READY), plug in the drive and follow the prompts. Don't turn the car off until it finishes.
Screen frozen or Bluelink not responding
  1. Try Settings → Data/Network → Reconfigure first.
  2. If the screen stays frozen, use the pinhole RESET button for 5–10 seconds, per Hyundai's reset page. Holding Map + Setup for 10 seconds also resets some systems.
  3. Settings and presets usually stay. Bluelink may take a few minutes to reconnect.

Hyundai's reset page is general. The exact reset buttons for the IONIQ 5 aren't confirmed, so check your owner's manual if these don't match your car.

Bluelink plans
  1. 2024+ (Bluelink+): Basic features are free. Advanced features are free for the original owner. Certified pre-owned buyers get Advanced free for one year.
  2. 2022–2023 (legacy Bluelink): a paid monthly plan. Original complimentary period not confirmed
  3. Map and multimedia OTA updates are free for 3 years, then paid. Safety and vehicle-system updates stay free.
